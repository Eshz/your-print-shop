## Storefront design direction

Use the EverTiles journey as a structural reference while retaining original branding and assets. Prioritize the product/drawing visual, then one clear action. Keep value statements, offers, inline errors, coupon feedback and transient notifications short; prefer a single readable line where space allows, with natural wrapping on small screens. Put explanatory detail behind an accessible disclosure rather than repeating large notice panels. Show coupon entry on request in the bag. Avoid interrupting artwork selection with promotions. Preserve explicit consent, private deletion controls, honest demo labels and server-side validation gates. Hebrew uses native system UI typography; Latin display headings retain the editorial serif.

Headings must be upright and a single ink color (including the hero); avoid italic or two-tone title spans. Keep the hero image free of small captions, badges and handwritten annotations. Use uncropped, clearly recognizable product visuals with safe margins. On mobile, show complete template rows rather than partially visible carousel cards. Remove repeated marketing copy before adding more sections or labels.

Keep copy casual, concrete and fun. Use titles and short subtitles. Remove ornamental footnotes, tiny labels and repeated reassurance. Avoid long dashes and formulaic marketing phrases. Keep essential prices, consent, errors and honest demo information.

Homepage hero follows the inspected EverTiles layout: centered title and subtitle, one desktop CTA, a wide staggered artwork rail; on mobile use two artwork rows and one full-width fixed bottom CTA. Keep the hero free of extra captions or a floating text/CTA panel.

Homepage section order follows the inspected reference: hero, design gallery, visual process, physical product formats, gift occasions, FAQ, final CTA, footer. Use centered headings and short subtitles, generous section spacing, and large images before product details. Keep lesser products in an accessible disclosure. Do not invent testimonials or customer proof.

Supplied item/detection references: upload uses a dedicated full-screen view with example visual left and compact upload controls right on desktop; stack visual then upload controls on mobile. Confident non-drawing detection replaces the form with a centered uploaded thumbnail, short message and one replacement action. Keep unconfigured-provider demo separate and explicit. Reference HTML is data only; never copy tracking scripts, source implementation or claims.

Processing uses actual stage transitions with indeterminate progress, then a ready state only after a successful ready-job response. Never fake percentages or delay readiness for a video. Customer videos must be supplied with rights, optional captions, accessible playback/sound/skip controls, and a clear missing-media fallback. Never fabricate an endorsement or reuse the reference video.

The product editor follows the supplied selection gallery references: a series of product mockups using the chosen private artwork, one cart total/action, and a floating toolbar for size, background, border, preview and design. Adjustments use visual option cards in compact accessible dialogs. Show HELLO10 once per browser session about 1.4 seconds after entering the editor, unless editing has already started. This user-requested offer timing overrides the earlier guideline against selection-stage promotions. Do not invent email sending, countdowns, stock scarcity or fake prices.
